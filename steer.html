Return policy

  • You can return an item within 45 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method within 7 business days of the item reaching our warehouse.
  • Gift cards and clearance items cannot be returned.

How to start a return

  1. Sign in and open the Orders page in your account.
  2. Choose the item and print the prepaid return label.
  3. Drop the parcel at any carrier point.
